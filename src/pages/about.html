An interior design and build company in Chennai with its own factory — for more than 20 years.

Our story

Twenty years,one workshop.

GM Modulars began as Maps Modular, and over more than two decades has grown into an interior company that designs and builds homes, villas, corporate offices and commercial spaces across Tamil Nadu.

What hasn’t changed is how we work. We run our own factory and our own team: modular furniture and kitchens, wooden doors and custom woodwork are made on our machinery, with laminates, edge banding and finishing done in-house.
