How we build

Four layers,one workshop.

Every panel we make goes through the same steps in our own factory. Doing each one ourselves is how we control the fit and the finish.

  • 01

    Carcass board

    Cut to size on our machinery, to the measurements taken on your site.

  • 02

    Laminate

    Pressed onto the board in-house, in the finish you choose.

  • 03

    Edge banding

    Every exposed edge sealed and trimmed by our team — clean to look at, protected against moisture.

  • 04

    Finish & hardware

    Final finishing and fittings, checked before anything leaves the factory.
